  1. pcmtap

pcmtap#

Record what an app (or the whole Mac) is playing, as PCM, via Core Audio process taps.

>>> from pcmtap import record
>>> record("song.wav", 30, app="Spotify", mute=True)
PosixPath('song.wav')

For streaming, Tap yields chunks of interleaved float32 frames, and pcmtap.stream composes over them (skip the leading silence, take N seconds). macOS 14.2+; the first recording asks for the System Audio Recording permission (request_permission(), pcmtap permission --request).

class pcmtap.Tap(app=None, *, pid=None, exclude=False, mute=False, seconds=None)[source]#

Bases: object

Capture what processes play, as an iterator of PCM chunks (bytes).

Parameters:

  • app (Union[str, Sequence[str], None]) – Bundle id(s) or app name(s) to record; all of their processes are included ("com.spotify.client" also matches its helper processes).

  • pid (Union[int, Sequence[int], None]) – Process id(s) to record.

  • exclude (bool) – Record everything except the selected processes.

  • mute (bool) – Silence the recorded processes on the speakers while recording.

  • seconds (float | None) – Stop after this long (otherwise when closed).

With no app and no pid, records every process on the Mac.

pcmtap.record(path, seconds=None, *, app=None, pid=None, exclude=False, mute=False, skip_leading_silence=False, silence_threshold=0.001, silence_timeout_s=30.0, until=None, bitrate=None)[source]#

Record what app (default: every process) plays into path; returns the path.

>>> record("out.wav", 10, app="Spotify")
Parameters:

  • path (str | Path) – Output file; .wav needs nothing, other formats need ffmpeg.

  • seconds (float | None) – How long to record (of sound, if skip_leading_silence). With neither seconds nor until, records until interrupted (Ctrl-C).

  • app (Union[str, Sequence[str], None]) – Which processes to record; see pcmtap.tap.Tap.

  • pid (Union[int, Sequence[int], None]) – Which processes to record; see pcmtap.tap.Tap.

  • exclude (bool) – Which processes to record; see pcmtap.tap.Tap.

  • mute (bool) – Which processes to record; see pcmtap.tap.Tap.

  • skip_leading_silence (bool) – Start the file at the first sound, waiting up to silence_timeout_s for it.

  • until (Optional[Callable[[], bool]]) – Stop as soon as this returns true (checked about every 10 ms).

  • bitrate (str | None) – For lossy formats, e.g. "320k".

Return type:

Path
