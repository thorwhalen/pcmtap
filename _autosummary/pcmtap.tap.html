pcmtap.tap#

A live tap on what processes are playing: a context manager yielding PCM chunks.

>>> with Tap(app="Spotify", mute=True) as tap:
...     print(tap.format)
...     for chunk in tap:          # bytes of interleaved float32 frames
...         ...

Tap runs the native helper (pcmtap.helper_build) as a subprocess and reads its stdout. Chunks always hold whole frames.

class pcmtap.tap.Tap(app=None, *, pid=None, exclude=False, mute=False, seconds=None)[source]#

Bases: object

Capture what processes play, as an iterator of PCM chunks (bytes).

Parameters:

  • app (Union[str, Sequence[str], None]) – Bundle id(s) or app name(s) to record; all of their processes are included ("com.spotify.client" also matches its helper processes).

  • pid (Union[int, Sequence[int], None]) – Process id(s) to record.

  • exclude (bool) – Record everything except the selected processes.

  • mute (bool) – Silence the recorded processes on the speakers while recording.

  • seconds (float | None) – Stop after this long (otherwise when closed).

With no app and no pid, records every process on the Mac.
